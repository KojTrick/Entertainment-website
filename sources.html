About our news feed

The stories on this site are collected automatically from public RSS feeds of Ghanaian and Nigerian entertainment sites, big international entertainment publications, and Google News searches. They are merged, de-duplicated and sorted newest first. The page checks for new stories every 10 minutes while it is open.

Big tiles, grids and the slider only show pictures that are at least 900 pixels wide (1200 for the slider's preferred pictures). A story with a smaller picture appears without one.
